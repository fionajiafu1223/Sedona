Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Log in with the same account, open any membership screen and tap "Restore purchases".

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Live In The End. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal.

How do I delete my account?
On the home screen, tap the avatar button → "Delete account". Your account and all cloud data are deleted right away.

A character said something inappropriate.
Long-press the message in the chat and choose to report it. We review every report.

Are the characters real people?
No. Characters and messages are generated by AI for personal entertainment and self-development.

常见问题

换了新手机，怎么恢复会员？
用同一个账号登录，打开任意会员页面，点「恢复购买」。

怎么取消订阅？
在 iPhone 上打开「设置」→ 你的名字 →「订阅」→ Live In The End。在当期结束前至少 24 小时取消，就不会续费。

怎么删除账号？
在首页点头像按钮 →「删除账号」，账号和全部云端数据会立即删除。

角色说了不恰当的话怎么办？
在聊天里长按这条消息，选择举报。每条举报我们都会查看。

角色是真人吗？
不是。角色和消息由 AI 生成，仅供个人娱乐和自我成长。
