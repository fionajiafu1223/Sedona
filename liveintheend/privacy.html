隐私政策

最近更新：2026年10月

Live In The End（"本应用"）由个人开发者独立开发和运营。本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、如何使用和保存，以及你拥有的权利。

一、账号信息

使用本应用需要用邮箱和密码注册账号。我们保存你的邮箱、加密后的密码、语言和时区设置，用于登录和同步你的内容。

二、你创作的内容

为了在不同设备上同步，并让 AI 角色生成消息，以下内容会保存在我们的云端数据库和存储空间（由 Supabase 提供）：

  • "人生剧本"中的主角、场景和人物设定，以及你上传的头像
  • 你和角色之间的聊天、短信、邮件，以及 AI 生成的消息和世界事件
  • 你在聊天中发给角色的语音和照片
  • "愿景板"中的图片和描述
  • "感恩日记"的内容和心情记录
"潜意识沟通室"中录制的声音、导入的音乐和混音作品会压缩后备份到你的云端账号，只有你自己能访问，用于换设备时自动恢复。删除账号时会一起删除。

三、第三方 AI 和语音服务

为了生成角色、消息和语音，相关内容会发送给以下服务商处理。我们只发送完成该功能所需的内容：

  • DeepSeek：根据剧本生成人物、消息和事件；为角色联网搜索真实生活细节
  • Microsoft Azure：生成角色聊天回复；识别你发送的照片和愿景板图片；把你的语音转成文字；为潜意识沟通室朗读肯定语
  • ElevenLabs：把角色的消息合成为语音；备用的语音转文字
  • Open-Meteo：根据剧本里的城市获取天气，让角色知道当地天气（只发送城市，不发送你的位置）

这些服务商按各自的隐私政策处理数据。请不要在剧本或聊天中填写身份证号、银行卡号等敏感信息。

订阅和付款

订阅通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成，付款信息由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡信息。我们使用 RevenueCat 管理订阅状态，它会收到你的匿名账号 ID 和购买记录（商品、时间、到期日），用于判断你的会员是否有效。

举报内容

如果你在聊天中长按并举报一条 AI 生成的消息，我们会保存这条消息的文字和角色名称，用于查看和改进内容。

四、设备权限

  • 麦克风：在你给角色发语音、和角色语音通话、或在潜意识沟通室录音时使用
  • 相册 / 相机：在你上传头像、愿景板图片、或给角色发照片时使用
  • 通知：用于提醒你角色发来的新消息，可在系统设置中关闭

五、我们不做的事

  • 不展示广告，不与广告商共享任何信息
  • 不使用第三方统计或行为追踪工具
  • 不出售或出租你的个人信息和内容

六、保存期限和删除

你的内容在账号存续期间保存。你可以随时在 App 首页左上角「登录/切换账号」→「删除账号」中删除账号，账号和全部云端数据（剧本、聊天记录、愿景板、感恩日记、上传的照片和语音）会立即永久删除。也可以发邮件到 fionajiafu1223@gmail.com 申请删除。

七、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其信息。

八、政策更新

本政策可能随功能更新而调整，重大变更会在本页面说明。

九、联系方式

如有任何疑问，请联系：fionajiafu1223@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Live In The End ("the app") is developed and operated by an independent developer. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used and stored, and your rights.

1. Account information

You need to sign up with an email and password to use the app. We store your email, your encrypted password, and your language and time zone settings to log you in and sync your content.

2. Content you create

To sync across devices and let AI characters write to you, the following is stored in our cloud database and storage (provided by Supabase):

  • Your protagonist, scenes and characters in the Life Script, and any photos you upload for them
  • Your chats, texts and emails with characters, plus AI-generated messages and world events
  • Voice messages and photos you send to characters in chat
  • Images and descriptions on your Vision Board
  • Your Gratitude Journal entries and moods
Recordings, imported music and mixes in the Subliminal Studio are compressed and backed up to your cloud account, accessible only to you, so they can be restored on a new device. They are deleted together with your account.

3. Third-party AI and voice services

To create characters, messages and voices, relevant content is sent to these providers for processing. We only send what each feature needs:

  • DeepSeek: generating characters, messages and events from your script; web searches for real-life details for characters
  • Microsoft Azure: generating character chat replies; describing photos you send and Vision Board images; transcribing your voice messages; reading affirmations aloud in the Subliminal Studio
  • ElevenLabs: turning characters' messages into speech; backup voice transcription
  • Open-Meteo: fetching the weather for cities in your script so characters know the local weather (only the city is sent, never your location)

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. Please don't enter sensitive information such as ID or bank card numbers in your script or chats.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. Payment details are handled by Apple, and we never see your card information. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status; it receives your anonymous account ID and purchase records (product, date, expiry) to check whether your membership is active.

Reporting content

If you long-press and report an AI-generated message in a chat, we store that message's text and the character's name so we can review and improve the content.

4. Device permissions

  • Microphone: when you send a voice message to a character, talk with a character on a voice call, or record in the Subliminal Studio
  • Photos / Camera: when you upload a profile photo, a Vision Board image, or send a photo to a character
  • Notifications: to let you know about new messages from characters; you can turn these off in system settings

5. What we don't do

  • No ads, and no sharing of information with advertisers
  • No third-party analytics or behavior tracking
  • We never sell or rent your personal information or content

6. Retention and deletion

Your content is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the app's home screen: "Log in / Switch account" → "Delete account". Your account and all cloud data (script, chats, Vision Board, Gratitude Journal, uploaded photos and voice messages) are permanently deleted right away. You can also request deletion by emailing fionajiafu1223@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as features are updated. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions, contact: fionajiafu1223@gmail.com
