退款政策

最后更新：2026年10月5日

1. 会员方案

Freed 会员提供月度、季度、年度三种方案。通过网站或安卓 App 订阅的会员会在每个周期结束时按订阅时显示的价格自动续费，直到你取消为止。

2. 7 天免费试用

新用户订阅时可享 7 天免费试用，试用期间不扣费。试用结束前 7 天我们会发邮件提醒你；如果在试用结束前取消，不会产生任何费用。试用结束后将自动按所选方案扣费。

3. 随时取消

  • 你可以随时通过每封账单邮件中的「管理订阅」链接自助取消，或发邮件联系我们代为取消。
  • 取消后不会再续费，已付费的会员可以继续使用到当前周期结束。

4. 续费提醒

每次自动续费前，我们会通过邮件提前提醒你；扣款成功后也会发送付款收据。

5. 退款

  • 忘记取消的续费：如果自动续费后你没有使用会员功能，可在扣款后 7 天内联系我们申请全额退款。
  • 重复扣款或扣款错误：经核实后全额退款。
  • 其他情况：已开始的会员周期一般不按比例退款，但如有特殊情况，欢迎联系我们，我们会逐一处理。
  • 退款将原路退回到你付款时使用的账户（如支付宝或银行卡），到账时间以支付机构为准，通常为 5–10 个工作日。

6. iOS 用户

通过 iPhone / iPad App Store 购买的会员由 Apple 处理扣款和退款，请通过 Apple 的 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

7. 联系我们

退款、取消或任何扣款问题，请发邮件至 fionajiafu1223@gmail.com，请附上付款邮箱和扣款日期，我们会尽快回复。

Refund Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Membership plans

Freed membership is offered as monthly, quarterly and yearly plans. Memberships purchased on our website or Android app renew automatically at the end of each period, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.

2. 7-day free trial

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial with no charge during the trial. We email you a reminder 7 days before the trial ends. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged. Otherwise, your selected plan is charged automatically when the trial ends.

3. Cancel anytime

  • You can cancel anytime using the "Manage subscription" link in any billing email, or by emailing us.
  • After cancelling, you will not be charged again and keep access until the end of the current paid period.

4. Renewal reminders

We email you before each automatic renewal, and send a receipt after every successful charge.

5. Refunds

  • Forgot to cancel: if you have not used the membership after an automatic renewal, contact us within 7 days of the charge for a full refund.
  • Duplicate or incorrect charges: fully refunded once verified.
  • Other cases: started periods are generally not refunded pro rata, but please contact us and we will review each case individually.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (e.g. Alipay or card). Timing depends on the payment provider, usually 5–10 business days.

6. iOS users

Purchases made through the Apple App Store are billed and refunded by Apple. Please request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

7. Contact us

For refunds, cancellations or any billing question, email fionajiafu1223@gmail.com with your payment email and charge date. We will reply as soon as possible.
